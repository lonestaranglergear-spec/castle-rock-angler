06 — Regulations Center

Check before you fish.

Pyramid Lake is on the Pyramid Lake Paiute Reservation and is regulated by the Tribe, not the State of Nevada — a Nevada fishing license does not cover it. The full regulations center organizes license, season, size/bag limit and special-rule categories in one place. Always verify current regulations with official Nevada and Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe sources.
